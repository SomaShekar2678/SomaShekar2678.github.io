Original downloads:
- https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/2k_earth_daymap.jpg
- https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/2k_earth_nightmap.jpg
- https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/2k_earth_clouds.jpg

Changes: JPEG converted to WebP; day and night retained at 2048×1024 (quality 86); clouds resized to 1024×512 (quality 80). Day map desaturated slightly in shader; sunlight, terminator, cloud shadows and atmospheric limb added in the renderer. Rendered derivatives also appear in public/media/journey/space.webp and fix.webp.

All spacecraft, antenna, PCB and vehicle geometry, material-detail textures, synthetic spectrum and LiDAR environment are original code authored for this portfolio. They are representative illustrations, not manufacturer CAD, photographs of project hardware, captured sensor data or validated physical simulations. No third-party satellite model is used. General spacecraft anatomy was checked against the FAA's navigation-satellite description:
https://www.faa.gov/about/office_org/headquarters_offices/ato/service_units/techops/navservices/gnss/gps/spacesegments

No external asset host is requested by the deployed renderer.
