Earth imagery

Earth Day Map, Earth Night Map and Earth Clouds by Solar System Scope, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. The source maps are based on NASA imagery and data.

We converted the maps to WebP, reduced the cloud map resolution and adjusted colour in the renderer. Lighting, cloud shadows and an atmospheric limb are added in real time. The same maps appear in the orbital and positioning scene posters. These adaptations do not imply endorsement.

Original engineering illustrations

The navigation spacecraft, antenna array, processing board and sensor vehicle are representative models created for this portfolio. They do not identify a particular operational satellite or reproduce the actual project hardware.

The antenna response uses the portfolio’s array model. Radio wave overlays, spectrum surfaces and ranging lines are explanatory illustrations. The street point cloud is produced by ray intersections in a synthetic environment; it is not a captured dataset. Scene scales are illustrative, and no positioning accuracy or hardware performance is implied.
